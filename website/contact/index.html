Request access.

The first public-site CTA is intentionally simple: a clear way to reach the team, describe your use case, and ask for access without introducing a new internal admin surface or a hastily built public signup backend.

Primary path

Email the team

For the current v1 pass, the production-safe contact path is direct email. It is simple, deployable today, and does not require exposing any internal APIs or bolting a half-finished waitlist service onto the public site.
